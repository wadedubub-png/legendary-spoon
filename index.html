PLAYER DATA

Settings

Bodyweight is stored once here and selects the matching published 10 kg comparison cohort for relative-strength standards. Changing it can change your benchmark cohort. The reference group selects the comparison dataset, not an identity label.

Standards transparency: bench, squat, deadlift and overhead press use the published GymStreak self-reported gym-lifter bodyweight cohorts (25th/50th/75th/90th percentiles; nearest 10 kg cohort). Supported accessory lifts try FitnessVolt's separate Symmetric Strength gym dataset through its public strength-standards API; competition results are never mixed into gym standards. Unsupported accessories and machine exercises use first-session personal baselines. Published bodyweight tests use OpenProgression's trained-population benchmarks, which combine several sources and are not a pure gym-only sample; unsupported bodyweight movements use personal baselines when no comparable benchmark exists. Where the source has fewer than seven levels, the final extra milestone is explicitly extrapolated. Rep conversion uses Epley for GymStreak lifts and Wathan for FitnessVolt lifts over 6–15 reps; 11–15 rep estimates are less certain, and the GymStreak source itself was built from 1–10 rep sets.

Backups let you transfer your workouts, cardio, XP, baselines and achievements between versions of the standalone app. Importing replaces the current ASCENSION data.

Benchmark data sources: GymStreak gym-lifter standards; OpenProgression (MIT); Powered by FitnessVolt (CC BY 4.0); Running Level.
